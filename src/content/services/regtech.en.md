Regulatory enforcement cannot scale through manual inspection. As the European Union tightens Ecodesign requirements and expands the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR), manufacturers, retailers, and public bodies face an increasingly complex compliance landscape.

I design and build software engines that bridge official directives and production data systems:

- **Algorithmic Regulatory Validation:** Implementing official European testing formulas, energy class boundary thresholds, and tolerance calculations directly in code to detect declared discrepancies automatically.
- **EPREL Integration & Ingest Pipelines:** Automated connectors to sync, query, and validate compliance data against the European Product Registry for Energy Labelling (EPREL) at scale.
- **Digital Product Passport (DPP) Readiness:** Designing the data architectures, interoperability layers, and schema transformations required for upcoming ESPR traceability standards.
- **Audit-Ready Evidence Trails:** Generating reproducible, timestamped technical compliance reports that eliminate human error during product verification audits.
