Energy efficiency is won or lost at the physical asset. Meters, circuits and gateways produce most of the raw signal a sustainability programme needs, and almost none of it is usable without engineering.

I build the path from field to dashboard end to end:

- **Field-to-dashboard ownership:** hardware selection, gateway configuration, protocol integration (Express, MQTT) and the service layer that receives it.
- **Telemetry modelling:** time-series ingest, per-site normalisation and comparison across a fleet of buildings with different meters and tariffs.
- **Remote control, not just reading:** command paths for switching loads and circuits, with the audit trail that a critical facility requires.
- **Dashboards for non-engineers:** reporting that facilities and energy teams use without training, replacing manual meter rounds and after-the-fact billing analysis.
