High-impact sustainability and regulatory platforms require scalable, low-overhead infrastructure that empowers domain experts without accumulating technical debt.

I provide end-to-end software engineering and systems architecture for data-intensive initiatives:

- **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Designing multi-region platforms that isolate local configurations, national energy criteria, and regional branding while sharing a unified core engine.
- **Bespoke Internal Tooling:** Building tailored CMS backends, verification interfaces, and review dashboards that make complex database workflows frictionless for researchers and market analysts.
- **Zero-Maintenance CI/CD & Cloud Infrastructure:** Modern infrastructure management using automated deployment pipelines (GitHub Actions, Cloudflare Pages/Workers, containerized backends) optimized for maximum uptime, strict security, and minimal operational overhead.
- **API Architecture & Data Distribution:** High-performance REST/GraphQL APIs and static generation strategies that deliver instant catalog response times to thousands of daily users.
