The biggest blind spot in energy market surveillance is identity mismatch: official regulatory databases register technical model identifiers, while e-commerce and retail channels use unstructured commercial names, localized variations, and missing barcodes.

I build automated resolution pipelines and observability platforms that close this gap:

- **Commercial-to-Regulatory Mapping:** High-precision heuristic and deterministic algorithms that match unstandardized brand and model strings to standard product identifiers (**GTIN / EAN**).
- **Market Observability Pipelines:** Aggregating and cross-referencing multi-source product feeds (regulatory registries, retail scrapers, and market intelligence APIs) into a unified telemetry stream.
- **Discrepancy Detection:** Automated alerts highlighting mismatches between retail claims (e.g., promotional energy labels on e-commerce sites) and certified regulatory filings in EPREL.
- **Market Penetration Analytics:** Tracking the real-world retail availability, pricing dynamics, and sales presence of best-in-class energy-efficient appliances.
